# Verified progress achievements

## Purpose

InsightOS rewards useful setup and repeatable work without turning the product
into a points game. The deterministic intelligence layer grants achievements;
AI may later help explain them but cannot create or approve one.

## Rule contract

Every grant stores the rule key and version, location or organization scope,
qualifying time, earned time, evidence payload, and evidence digest. The unique
rule-and-scope constraint makes provider retries, event replay, and repeated
page loads safe.

The first release contains three location-scoped foundation rules:

1. `foundation.location_ready@1.0` requires an active location with a business
   name, website, and saved service area.
2. `foundation.first_live_sync@1.0` requires a supported live connection and a
   recorded successful update. A connection toggle without a successful update
   is insufficient.
3. `foundation.first_trustworthy_baseline@1.0` requires a governed action
   measurement with an available primary metric, versioned measurement
   contract, matching scope, and source evidence.

Checkbox completion, forecasts, AI text, demo data, credits, scans, and page
views do not qualify for these rules. Verified-result achievements remain
disabled until a fresh post-action measurement can prove the result in the
same scope.

The second release adds three location-scoped healthy-habit rules:

1. `habit.first_weekly_plan@1.0` requires a completed weekly occurrence with at
   least one required checklist step and every required step saved as `done`.
2. `habit.three_weekly_plans@1.0` requires qualifying weekly plans in three
   different governed period keys. The periods do not have to form a brittle
   streak, so a quiet week, vacation, or provider delay does not erase useful
   work.
3. `habit.first_monthly_plan@1.0` applies the same required-step rule to one
   completed monthly occurrence.

These are work badges, not improvement claims. Each grant saves the occurrence,
period, action, completion time, and required-step count. A status flag without
the completed required steps is insufficient.

Two organization-scoped multi-location rules are also active when an
organization has at least two active locations:

1. `multi_location.all_locations_ready@1.0` requires every active location to
   have a business name, website, and service area.
2. `multi_location.all_locations_current@1.0` requires every active location to
   have a successful supported connection update within a 21-day freshness
   window. The grant saves every qualifying location and connection. One fresh
   location cannot hide an outdated or disconnected location.

Organization grants appear beside the selected location's history with the
scope label `All active locations`; they are stored separately from location
grants and remain tenant isolated.

## Correction policy

Grants are append-only history. A governed correction sets `corrected_at` and a
human-readable `correction_reason`; it does not delete the record. Corrected
grants remain in history but are excluded from active counts and next-milestone
progress. A rule change requires a new rule version and does not silently
rewrite earlier evidence.

## API and customer experience

- `POST /api/v1/engagement/achievements/evaluate?campaign_id=...` evaluates
  current evidence and materializes any new idempotent grants.
- `GET /api/v1/engagement/achievements?campaign_id=...` reads scoped history.
- `PATCH /api/v1/engagement/achievement-preferences` saves optional milestone
  messages and progress-reminder preferences without deleting history.

The Next Steps page shows a compact summary, one next milestone, location and
date, and plain-language proof. Celebration messages are dismissible and use a
non-animated accessible status surface.

Each new grant also records one idempotent, server-verified
`achievement.earned` product event. It is not generated by page views, button
clicks, or repeat evaluations and is kept separate from the useful-activity
counter so rollout analysis does not manufacture engagement.

## Future governed stages

Verified-result rules will require a new same-scope observation after completed
work. They remain disabled until the production measurement contract proves the
result and cannot be inferred from a habit or multi-location grant.
